# JoSAA Choice Filling Strategy Guide

## Safe / Target / Dream framework

- **Safe**: Your rank is comfortably better than the closing rank (large margin). High admission probability this round.
- **Target**: Your rank is close to closing rank — reasonable chance, especially in later rounds.
- **Dream**: Borderline or stretch — may open only if seats vacate or cutoffs move favourably.

## Branch vs college

- For **core engineering** goals (software, core tech): prioritize branch (CSE, IT, ECE) over marginal college brand.
- For **higher studies / research**: IIT/NIT tier and department culture matter more.
- For **placements**: Check department placement trends, not just institute average.

## IIT vs NIT vs IIIT

- **IITs**: Best overall brand; very competitive cutoffs.
- **NITs**: Strong value; home-state quota (HS) can help if your state matches.
- **IIITs**: Excellent for computing-focused programs; some are PPP/established IIITs with different fee structures.

## State quota advantage

If you are from the same state as an NIT, **HS quota** seats often have better (higher numeric) closing ranks than AI quota for the same program — use this in your strategy.

## Round progression

Cutoffs usually **increase** (worse) in early rounds and may stabilize by round 5–6. Do not assume round-1 closing equals final outcome.

## Common mistakes

1. Filling only dream colleges at the top.
2. Ignoring quota type (AI vs HS vs OS).
3. Not reading gender-neutral vs female-only labels.
4. Copying a friend's list without matching category/quota/rank.
