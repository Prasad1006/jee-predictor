# JoSAA Counselling FAQ

## What is JoSAA?

JoSAA (Joint Seat Allocation Authority) conducts centralized counselling for admission to IITs, NITs, IIITs, and other GFTIs based on JEE Main and JEE Advanced ranks.

## How does seat allocation work?

Seats are allocated in multiple rounds (typically 6). In each round you fill choices in order of preference. If a higher preference becomes available and you accept/freeze as per rules, you may move up. Lower preferences act as backups.

## Rank vs closing rank

A **lower rank number is better** (rank 1 is best). You are generally eligible for a seat if your rank is **better than or equal to** the published closing rank for that seat in a given round, category, quota, and gender.

## Quota types

- **AI (All India)**: Open to all candidates nationwide.
- **HS (Home State)**: For candidates whose home state matches the institute's state.
- **OS (Other State)**: For candidates from states other than the institute's state.

## Categories

Common seat categories: GENERAL (OPEN), OBC-NCL, SC, ST, EWS, and PwD variants. Always use the category shown on your JEE admit card / counselling registration.

## Freeze, Float, Slide

- **Freeze**: Accept allocated seat and exit further rounds.
- **Float**: Accept seat but remain eligible for **same institute, higher preference branch** in later rounds.
- **Slide**: Accept seat but remain eligible for **higher preference program at same institute**.

## Choice filling strategy

1. Put 2–3 **safe** choices at the top to avoid slipping to very low options.
2. Place **target** colleges in the middle — realistic upgrades.
3. Add **dream** options below targets — possible in later rounds if seats vacate.
4. Never leave large gaps without backups.
5. Prefer branch over college only when you are confident about that branch long-term.

## Female-only and gender-neutral seats

Some seats are **Female-only** or **Gender-Neutral**. Filter by the gender field that matches your counselling profile.

## Important disclaimer

Cutoffs change every year. Always verify on the official JoSAA portal (josaa.nic.in) before locking choices.
